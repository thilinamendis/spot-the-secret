    Spot the Secret

    People can hide secret messages inside photos. Your eyes can't see them, but the right tool can. Can you find all 10?

    1. Look. Each level shows two photos, A and B. One of them hides a secret.
    2. Use the tool. The Contrast Booster or the X-ray changes both photos at once. Move its slider and compare.
    3. Pick. Click "A hides the secret" or "B hides the secret". Each correct answer is worth 10 points.
    4. Beat the clock. You have 5 minutes for all 10 levels. When time runs out, the game ends.
